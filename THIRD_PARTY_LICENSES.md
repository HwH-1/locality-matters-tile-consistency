# Third-party code and data

## Code: JLR-CAKF reference operator (code/reference/)

The anisotropic-Kuwahara operator implementation in `code/reference/src/`
originates from the predecessor project released as
`TheVisualComputer_JLR_project.zip`. It is distributed under the MIT licence
reproduced verbatim in `code/reference/LICENSE`:

    MIT License
    Copyright (c) 2026 The JLR-CAKF authors

That notice and the full permission text are preserved in this release as
required by the licence. filters.py, methods.py and the remaining analysis
modules are copied without modification.

Two files are exceptions: metrics.py and structure.py in
code/reference/src/ are the paper's revised versions (identical to
code/tc/metrics.py and code/tc/structure.py), which override the JLR
copies. The revisions add an FSR guard when fewer than 100 flat pixels are
available and filter non-finite values in paired tests; they are required to
reproduce the released numbers. Both files remain under the MIT terms above.
